# Feedback

Open a [beta feedback issue](https://github.com/Tumub/natural-price/issues/new?template=beta-feedback.md)
on GitHub. No account there? Email the address in the release notes with
the same questions answered.

We do not want your name, your account on any shop, or a screenshot that
shows either. An email address is optional and only so we can ask a
follow-up question.

## The questions

1. Which site and which product page? (Address without the part after `?`.)
2. What did the panel say? Copy the sentence.
3. Was it right? If you checked in a private window, what did that show?
4. Did it change what you did: bought elsewhere, waited, used the private
   window, nothing?
5. Anything that looked wrong, slow, or in the way?
6. Optional: an email for a follow-up question.

## What happens with it

Answers to 1 to 3 go into the site's fixture and extractor when they show
a bug. Answer 4 is counted in the weekly summary. Issues are public; do not
put anything in them you would not want public.
